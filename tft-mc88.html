📖 How to use TFT MC88

1
Write your text
Type anything in the left box, just like a normal note.
2
Select it
Highlight the part you want to format.
3
Tap a tool
Choose Heading, Bold, List, Quote… from the toolbar.
"Hello" → Heading → becomes a title
4
Watch the preview
The right panel updates instantly — that's exactly how it will look.
5
Export it
Tap "Copy" to copy the raw HTML, or "Download" to save a ready .html file.
6
Open the downloaded file
Find it in your device's "Downloads" folder and tap it — it opens straight in your browser. You can rename it anything, e.g. index.html.
7
Use it in a real project
Move the file into your website folder. Want it styled or made interactive? Open it next in STYLEME MC88 or SCRIPTME MC88.

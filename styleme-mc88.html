📖 How STYLEME MC88 works
1
Tap any element
Tap directly on anything inside the preview above. A frame highlights it — that's now selected.
2
Style it
Use Color, Effects, Text or Page at the bottom to change it instantly. No two elements ever clash — each gets its own unique ID automatically.
3
Export your CSS
Tap "Export" to download styleme-export.css.
4
Connect it to your HTML
Put the downloaded file in your project folder, then add this line inside your page's <head>:
<link rel="stylesheet" href="styleme-export.css">
5
Bring your own page
Tap "Import" to paste HTML you made with TFT MC88, then style it here.
